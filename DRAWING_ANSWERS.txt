DRAWING-BASED ANSWERS
Cherokee plant | Prepared 2026-11-06

PURPOSE
Use this as a factual briefing from the drawings reviewed so far. It is an
answer sheet, not a list of questions to take back to the project team. State
what a drawing shows, cite the sheet, and distinguish it from field-verified
conditions. These drawings are not certified as-builts.

THE SHORT ANSWER
The one-lines show S and V on the same upstream normal-power system: 3XFMR-0021
feeds SWBD #6, which has a 200A branch to Building V and a branch toward
Building S labeled 800A, 3-pole, with 300A also shown at the protective device.
That establishes a shared normal source in the drawing set. It does not
establish that S and V share emergency generation. The 750kW generator
shown in the set is 3GEN-0015, whose ESG01 board feeds multiple N-building ATSs;
the reviewed sheets do not show it feeding S or V.

ANSWERS SUPPORTED BY THE DRAWINGS

1. S AND V NORMAL-POWER ARRANGEMENT
   Drawing 15-039-E073 (PDF page 49) shows 3XFMR-0021, rated 1500kVA, supplying
   SWBD #6, shown as 2000A minimum at 480/277V. The board has a 200A feeder to
   Building V (continuation 15-039-E078). The branch toward Building S
   (continuation 15-039-E076) is labeled 800A, 3-pole, with 300A also shown at
   the protective device. Report both markings as drawn; do not describe the
   branch as 800A of usable capacity. The drawings show separate building
   feeders downstream of a common transformer/switchboard. That is a common
   normal source, not proof of a shared emergency source.

2. V-BUILDING NORMAL DISTRIBUTION
   Drawing 15-039-E078 (PDF page 52) shows the SWBD #6 feeder serving panel
   3PAN-V-0001 / DP-1, rated 200A, 480/277V, 3-phase, 4-wire. The listed
   downstream loads include AHUs, cooling units, lighting, and general building
   loads. This sheet shows normal distribution; it does not identify a V
   generator or emergency ATS.

3. S-BUILDING NORMAL DISTRIBUTION
   Drawing 15-039-E073 routes a branch to Building S via drawing 15-039-E076.
   The branch device is marked 800A, 3-pole, with 300A also shown at the
   protective device. That continuation sheet is not present in the supplied
   71-page PDF. Drawing 15-039-E077 (PDF page 51) is a Building S panel
   3PAN-XXX1, rated 225A, 480/277V, 3-phase, 4-wire. Its listed loads include
   machine-shop equipment, an AHU, receptacles, a battery charger, and other
   building loads. The missing E076 sheet prevents a complete trace of the
   feeder and upstream S distribution from this PDF alone.

4. S/V EMERGENCY POWER IS NOT ESTABLISHED BY THESE SHEETS
   The S/V sheets cited above establish their normal-power arrangement, but do
   not show an S/V shared generator or emergency ATS. The previously reported
   shared S/V emergency arrangement remains unverified against the supplied
   drawings. Do not equate the common normal source with a common standby
   source.

5. BUILDING N STANDBY GENERATOR
   Drawing 15-039-E062 (PDF page 38) identifies 3GEN-0015 as a 750kW standby
   generator feeding switchboard 3GEN-0015-ESG01, shown as 1200A,
   480/277V, 3-phase, 4-wire, 25kAIC. Its outgoing feeders serve multiple
   ATSs, including ATS07 and ATS-N3-EB. This establishes the source shown on
   that one-line; it does not establish current field status, ownership, or
   unused generator capacity.

6. ATS-N3-EB SOURCE
   Drawing 15-039-E069 (PDF page 45) shows ATS-N3-EB's normal source as DP-7
   and its emergency source as 3GEN-0015-ESG01. The ATS is labeled 400A,
   480V, 3-phase, 3-wire, 3-pole, and "not a Level 1" transfer switch. Its
   load side feeds panel SPP-N3-EB. This is N-system evidence, not evidence
   that the N generator serves S or V.

7. ATS07 SOURCE CONFLICT
   The drawings conflict:
   - 15-039-E068 (PDF page 44) shows ATS07's normal source as DP-10 and its
     emergency source as 3GEN-0015-ESG01. It labels ATS07 1000A at 480V,
     3-phase, 3-wire, 3-pole, and "not a Level 1" transfer switch. The shown
     emergency panel supplies loads including AHU/exhaust equipment and UPS
     distribution.
   - 18-2038-E-50, "ADC Clinical Expansion Single Line Diagram," labels ATS07
     150A at 480V, 3-phase, 3-wire, with emergency power from an existing
     portable generator provided by others and located by the existing
     exterior generator. The feeder branch to ATS07 on 15-039-E062 also has
     400A and 150A markings.
   The drawings conflict on both ATS07's emergency source and rating. They
   alone do not reconcile whether this reflects revisions, separate equipment,
   or a field change. Report both references and do not declare the current
   source or rating until the ATS identity and installation are checked.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE SEPARATION
- The documented S/V normal-power tie is upstream at 3XFMR-0021 / SWBD #6.
- The supplied S/V normal one-lines do not document the emergency-power tie
  described in the earlier project narrative.
- The 750kW 3GEN-0015 is drawn as an N-building source. Its nameplate rating
  alone is not evidence that it can serve V or has spare capacity.
- The S and V branch markings are not measured demand or proof of available
  capacity; preserve the 800A / 300A S-device markings until their meaning is
  confirmed from the gear details or field labels.
- For a meeting: "The drawings establish a shared normal source for S and V,
  but they do not establish a shared S/V emergency source. The 750kW genset
  shown is assigned to N-system ATSs. ATS07's emergency source and rating are
  inconsistent between the plant and ADC expansion one-lines."
