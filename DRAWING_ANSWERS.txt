PURPOSE
Use this as a factual briefing from the drawings reviewed so far. It is an
answer sheet, not a list of questions to take back to the project team. State
what a drawing shows, cite the sheet, and distinguish it from field-verified
conditions. These drawings are not certified as-builts.

Sources: the 71-page MSOL set (MSOL-combined (1).pdf, drawings 15-039-E025 to
E107), the separate 18-2038-E-50 single line, and the one-page site plan
15-039-E001 (CherokeeSiteElect.pdf). The labels in these PDFs are stored as
annotation text; they were read from that text and checked against the
rendered sheets. PDF page numbers are 1-based.

THE SHORT ANSWER
The one-lines show S and V on the same upstream normal-power system: 3XFMR-0021
feeds SWBD #6, which has a 200A branch to Building V and a branch toward
Building S labeled 800A, 3-pole, with 300A also shown at the protective device.
That establishes a shared normal source in the drawing set. It does not
establish that S and V share emergency generation. The 750kW generator
shown in the set is 3GEN-0015, whose ESG01 board feeds multiple N-building ATSs;
the reviewed sheets do not show it feeding S or V.

The drawings do carry one specific lead to an emergency source at S/V: a
generator tagged 3GEN-002N. SWBD #6 has an 800A branch "TO 3GEN-002N-ATS08"
(E073), and panel 3PAN-0378 in the room labeled "BUILDING S MEZZANINE" has a
60A branch "TO 3GEN-002N-ATS10" (E028). On the site plan (E001, revision 4), a
3GEN-002N leader points to a generator-type unit in the yard, with CAM LOK BOX
#1 and #2 leaders to the same unit, beside the SWBD#6 and 3XFMR-0021
footprints. The ATS sheets (E099, E101) are not in the set and the generator
sheets (E102, E103) are blank placeholders. What 3GEN-002N serves, its rating,
and whether it is the S/V emergency source are therefore not established.

In this set, the normal-power links drawn between the N and S systems run from
N to S: DP-5 feeds the S mezzanine panel 3PAN-0378 (E028), and N's SWBD #5 has
a breaker marked "TO FUTURE 125A PANEL S BLDG" (E058). If ATS08 and ATS10 share
one generator (the set's tag convention suggests they do, but no sheet shows
it), that generator would tie the SWBD #6 system (S/V) to the DP-5 system (N).
None of this is field verified.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE SEPARATION
- The documented S/V normal-power tie is upstream at 3XFMR-0021 / SWBD #6.
  E078 places SWBD #6 "within Bldg P," and SWBD #6 also feeds a branch toward
  Bldg P (E073); the drawings do not say who owns Bldg P.
- The supplied S/V normal one-lines do not document the emergency-power tie
  described in the earlier project narrative. The one drawn lead is the
  3GEN-002N tag (E073 ATS08, E028 ATS10); the sheets that would show what it
  serves (E099, E101) are not in the set.
- Three drawn items could cross the planned ownership line and cannot be
  settled from this set:
  (a) 3GEN-002N, tied by tag to SWBD #6 (S/V) and to 3PAN-0378, which is fed
      from N's DP-5. If one generator serves both ATSs (inference), it is a
      shared emergency source across the line.
  (b) The N-to-S normal-power ties: DP-5 to 3PAN-0378 in the S mezzanine
      (E028), and SWBD #5 via 3PAN-0055 to the "future 125A panel S BLDG"
      (E058). They run from N toward S; no S-to-N tie was found.
  (c) The docking-station ATSs 3GEN-0003-ATS01 to ATS04. Their normal source
      (3SWBD-0009) shares an Ameren service and meter (3SWBD-0008) with N's
      SWBD #5, and their loads are on absent sheets (E108 to E111).
- The 750kW 3GEN-0015 is drawn as an N-building source. Its nameplate rating
  alone is not evidence that it can serve V or has spare capacity.
- The S and V branch markings are not measured demand or proof of available
  capacity; preserve the 800A / 300A S-device markings until their meaning is
  confirmed from the gear details or field labels.
- E001 does not letter its buildings, so it cannot by itself map yard
  equipment to S, V, or N. A building-lettered plan or a leader legend is
  needed before ownership can be mapped to the site plan.
- For a meeting: "The drawings establish a shared normal source for S and V,
  but not a shared S/V emergency source. A generator tagged 3GEN-002N is tied
  by tag to SWBD #6 and to a panel fed from N's DP-5, but its ATS sheets are
  not in the set. The 750kW genset is assigned to N-system ATSs. ATS07's
  emergency source and rating are inconsistent between the plant and ADC
  expansion one-lines."
